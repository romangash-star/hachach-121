SOURCE AND LICENCE TRAIL — raw sound effects
============================================

Nine raw files, all nine obtained from Zapsplat (https://www.zapsplat.com)
on a PAID Zapsplat subscription (Gold, £4.99/month), not the free tier.
Provenance as stated by Lion — the first five on 09 Sep 2026, the four
later takes confirmed the same day as coming from the same paid account,
within the download-time exemption.

ATTRIBUTION: NOT REQUIRED
-------------------------
The credit condition applies to Zapsplat's free Basic tier. A paid Gold or
Premium subscription removes it, and the removal is attached to the
DOWNLOAD rather than to the account: sounds downloaded while the
subscription was active stay licensed for attribution-free use for life,
in unlimited projects, even if the subscription later lapses.

So no Zapsplat credit line is owed for any of these nine files, and none
has been built. This supersedes two earlier notes in this file — the first
assumed the free Standard Licence and flagged a credit as outstanding; the
second held the four later takes open pending their provenance. Both are
closed. Nothing about attribution is outstanding.

NOT FROM ZAPSPLAT
-----------------
assets/sfx/complete.wav is built here, from the files above. Every transient
in it is one of ours; the reverb tail is a synthesised impulse response,
because nothing in the nine has any sustain to stretch. No third-party
material of any kind is in it.

WHY THE RAW FILES STAY IN THE REPO
----------------------------------
The processed files in assets/sfx/ are trimmed, resampled, loudness-matched
cuts. Without these originals there is no way to re-cut them, and no way to
show where they came from. Kept on Lion's instruction, 09 Sep 2026.

HOW THE LICENCE POSITION WAS CHECKED
------------------------------------
Zapsplat's own licence pages refuse automated fetches (HTTP 403), so the
tier terms above were read from search results quoting zapsplat.com's FAQ
and upgrade pages, corroborated by an independent licence guide. Three
sources agree on all three points: free requires credit, paid does not, and
the exemption follows the download for life. This is not a primary reading
of the licence document itself, and the account and download facts are as
Lion stated them rather than checked against Zapsplat's records.
